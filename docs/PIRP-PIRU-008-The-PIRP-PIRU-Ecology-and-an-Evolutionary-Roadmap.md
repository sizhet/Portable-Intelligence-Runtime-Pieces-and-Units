# PIRP-PIRU-008 — The PIRP/PIRU Ecology and an Evolutionary Roadmap

## Abstract

The previous articles introduced Portable Intelligence Runtime Pieces and Units as first-class intelligence objects and developed several associated mechanisms:

- pre-categorical intelligence,
- PIRP/PIRU duality,
- runtime anatomy and lifecycle,
- publishing as intelligence transfer,
- Open PIRPs,
- Relay Context,
- Knowledge and Relay CallingGraphs,
- Relay Localization,
- and Computational Growth Dispatch.

This article integrates these components into a broader **PIRP/PIRU Ecology**.

The central observation is practical:

> **The PIRP/PIRU ecosystem does not need to be invented from scratch.**

Much of its substrate already exists in:

- papers,
- repositories,
- issues,
- questions,
- code,
- experiments,
- benchmarks,
- RFCs,
- research notes,
- failed attempts,
- open problems,
- and human collaboration.

These artifacts already participate implicitly in a lifecycle resembling:

> **Produce -> Publish -> Discover -> Pickup -> Extend -> Validate -> Republish**

What is missing is primarily explicit structure.

In particular:

- explicit PIRP identity,
- machine-operable context,
- Known/Unknown boundaries,
- capability requirements,
- structural relations,
- Relay Context,
- growth lineage,
- and first-class AI participation.

This suggests an incremental evolutionary path rather than a disruptive replacement.

A preliminary roadmap is proposed:

```text
Level 0 — Existing Internet and Research Infrastructure
Level 1 — Explicit PIRP Metadata
Level 2 — AI PIRP Extraction
Level 3 — AI Relay Localization
Level 4 — Relay Graph
Level 5 — AI-Generated Open PIRPs
Level 6 — PIRP/PIRU Composition
Level 7 — Computational Growth Intelligence
````

The resulting ecosystem does not require every participant to understand or join the entire intelligence architecture.

Its participation principle can remain extremely simple:

> **No participant needs to join the whole intelligence system. Each participant only needs to pick up one PIRP.**

At sufficient scale, this creates a distributed intelligence ecology in which humans, AIs, tools, algorithms, repositories, and runtime units continuously publish, discover, relay, validate, compose, and grow portable intelligence.

> **Open problems, failed attempts, counter-evidence, and leftovers are first-class growth resources.**

## 107. Principle 7 — Collective Learning Includes Relay Learning

> **The system should learn not only what is known, but how intelligence moves productively between participants.**

---

## 108. Principle 8 — Growth Is Structural

> **More artifacts are not sufficient; useful growth requires better relations, capabilities, compositions, localization, and continuation.**

---

## 109. Principle 9 — PIRP and PIRU Must Coexist

> **PIRP preserves emergence and openness; PIRU provides stability and engineering control.**

---

## 110. Principle 10 — The Ecology Should Remain Open-Ended
