# START HERE — PIRP / PIRU

## Portable Intelligence Runtime Pieces and Units

### A 5–10 Minute Guide to Portable, Relayable, and Growing Intelligence

---

## 1. The Core Question

Modern computing gives us many useful abstractions:

```text
State
Function
Class
Service
Agent
CallingGraph
CallingPath
CCC
DNA
Policy
Memory
````

But Structural Intelligence increasingly exposes another question:

> **What is the bounded piece of intelligence that actually exists, acts, travels, and grows at runtime?**

A runtime node may produce more than a value or trigger.

It may produce a composite structure containing:

```text
Structure
+
State
+
Computation
+
Trigger
+
Memory
+
Policy
+
Evidence
+
Interface
+
Growth Potential
```

This repository calls such emerging portable runtime intelligence a:

> **PIRP — Portable Intelligence Runtime Piece**

When sufficiently bounded, typed, validated, interfaced, and governed, it can become a:

> **PIRU — Portable Intelligence Runtime Unit**

---

# 2. PIRP in One Minute

A PIRP is a recognizable piece of intelligence that can potentially be:

```text
identified
carried
published
localized
continued
executed
observed
composed
validated
grown
```

PIRP is deliberately a **Piece**, not necessarily a formal Unit.

Why?

Because intelligence may appear before we fully understand how to classify it.

The guiding principle is:

> **Intelligence precedes its computational classification.**

Therefore:

```text
Runtime Reality
      |
      v
Emergent Intelligence
      |
      v
     PIRP
```

PIRP preserves the intelligence first.

Formalization can come later.

---

# 3. PIRU in One Minute

A PIRU is a more formalized intelligence unit.

It typically has clearer:

```text
Identity
Boundary
Type
Interface
Runtime Contract
Validation
Policy
Version
Lifecycle
```

Thus:

```text
PIRP
 |
 | observe
 | understand
 | find boundary
 | validate
 | type
 | extract interface
 v
PIRU
```

A PIRU is easier to:

```text
execute
reuse
compose
govern
dispatch
validate
```

---

# 4. PIRP and PIRU Are a Duality

PIRP and PIRU are not competing names.

They represent two complementary orientations.

| PIRP                   | PIRU                  |
| ---------------------- | --------------------- |
| Discovery              | Formalization         |
| Emerging               | Defined               |
| Open boundary          | Explicit boundary     |
| Heterogeneous          | Normalized            |
| Growth-first           | Interface-aware       |
| Identity emerging      | Identity declared     |
| Exploratory validation | Systematic validation |
| Open composition       | Governed composition  |

A useful shorthand is:

> **PIRP protects emerging intelligence.
> PIRU makes understood intelligence engineerable.**

---


# 7. Portable Means More Than Software Portability

Traditional portability asks:

> Can this code execute somewhere else?

Portable Intelligence asks:

> **Can another intelligence receive this piece, reconstruct enough of its context, continue its computation, and grow from where the previous intelligence stopped?**

This gives several levels:

```text
Computational Portability
          |
          v
Contextual Portability
          |
          v
Agent Portability
          |
          v
Civilizational Portability
```

A PIRP may move across:

```text
Machine -> Machine
Runtime -> Runtime
Human   -> Human
Human   -> AI
AI      -> Human
AI      -> AI
Generation -> Generation
```

The central idea is:

> **A PIRP is not merely portable across runtimes; it may be portable across intelligences.**

Once intelligence can be portable, publication becomes more than document distribution.

```text
Agent-A
   |
   v
Externalize Intelligence

   v

   v
Shared Intelligence Space
   |
   v
Discover
   |
   v

   |
   v
Continue Computation
```

This suggests:

> **Publish = inject a portable intelligence structure into a larger intelligence runtime.**

A paper, repository, DOI, algorithm, experiment, or structured question can potentially function as a PIRP.

The important property is not file format.

It is **continuation potential**.

# 9. A Problem Can Be Intelligence

One of the most important extensions is the **Open PIRP**.

Suppose we know:

```text
Known
  |
  v
Current Boundary
  |
  v
Unknown
```

If that boundary is sufficiently structured, the unresolved problem itself becomes portable intelligence.

An Open PIRP may contain:

```text
Problem
Context

Known
Unknown
Structural Gap

Evidence
Counter-Evidence

Constraints
Failed Attempts

Required Capability
Required Tools

Validation Conditions
Open Ends
```

The central proposition is:

> **A well-formed problem is executable intelligence whose next computation has not yet been completed.**

Therefore:

> **Unresolved PIRP does not mean incomplete intelligence.**

It means intelligence whose continuation remains open.

# 11. Relay

Suppose Agent-A reaches a boundary:

```text
Agent-A
   |
   v
Open PIRP-X
```

Another intelligence may continue it:

```text
Open PIRP-X
    |
    v
  Relay
    |
    v
Agent-B / AI / Tool / PIRU
    |
    v
Structural Delta
```

This is **Relay**.

Relay means:

> **Computation continues across an intelligence boundary.**

The receiver does not have to be another human.

It may be:

```text
Human
AI
Algorithm
Tool
Simulator
Database
PIRU
Team
Hybrid System
```

---

# 12. Relay Context Is Intelligence

It matters not only that a relay occurred.

We also want to know:

```text
Who received it?

Why?

Under what context?

Through which perspective?

Using which capability?

What changed?

What evidence appeared?

What new PIRP resulted?
```

This information is **Relay Context**.

Provenance asks:

> Where did this come from?

Relay Context asks:

> **Why did this intelligence become productive here, and what happened next?**

That history itself becomes reusable intelligence.

---

# 13. Two CallingGraphs

The framework distinguishes two related structures.

## Knowledge CallingGraph

Answers:

> **What intelligence relates to what intelligence?**

For example:

```text
PIRP-A
  |
  +---- extends ------> PIRP-B
  |
  +---- contradicts --> PIRP-C
  |
  +---- validates ----> PIRP-D
```

## Relay CallingGraph

Answers:

> **How did intelligence actually move and grow?**

For example:

```text
PIRP-A
  |
  v
Human-B
  |
  v
PIRP-C
  |
  v
AI-D
  |
  v
PIRP-E
```

Together they contribute to a:

> **Collective Intelligence CallingGraph**

# 14. Localization Also Has Two Forms

Structural Intelligence traditionally asks:

> **Where is the relevant intelligence?**

This is **Structural Localization**.

Examples:

```text
Find CCC
Find PIRU
Find CallingPath
Find Evidence
Find Policy
```

Portable Intelligence adds another question:

> **Who or what should continue this intelligence?**

This is **Relay Localization**.

Examples:

```text
Find Human
Find AI
Find Tool
Find PIRU
Find Team
Find Hybrid Configuration
```

---

# 15. The Best Relay May Be Unknown

Human collaboration often starts with:

```text
Who do I know?
```

But the most useful next intelligence may be outside the source agent's social graph.

Therefore:

> **Social Proximity is not Intelligence Proximity.**

The target may be:

> **organizationally distant but structurally near intelligence.**

This suggests searching through:

```text
Capability
Context
Tools
Past Relay Outcomes
Structural Similarity
Structural Complementarity
```

rather than only social relationships.

AI does not initially need to autonomously solve every PIRP.

It can first become:

```text
PIRP Extractor
Structurer
Indexer
Matcher
Counter-Evidence Finder
Relay Localizer
Relay Assistant
Relay Recorder
```

> **Help the PIRP find the next relay.**

This creates a natural route for AI participation in Collective Intelligence.

> **No participant needs to join the whole intelligence system. Each participant only needs to pick up one PIRP.**

PIRP/PIRU begins with a small question:

> **What is the runtime piece of intelligence?**

But following that question produces a much larger path.

If intelligence can become a bounded runtime piece, it can potentially become:

```text
Portable
   |
   v
Publishable
   |
   v
Discoverable
   |
   v
Relayable
   |
   v
Composable
   |
   v
Growable
```

And once many such pieces can move among:

```text
Humans
AIs
Algorithms
Tools
PIRUs
Organizations
Generations
```

the result is no longer merely a collection of intelligent components.

It begins to resemble an **Intelligence Ecology**.

The long-term transition is:

```text
Runtime Intelligence
        |
        v
Portable Intelligence
        |
        v
Relayable Intelligence
        |
        v
Collective Learning
        |
        v
Computational Growth Intelligence
```

The practical entry point, however, remains intentionally small:

> **Pick up one PIRP. Continue it. Produce one useful Structural Delta. Pass the intelligence forward.**
